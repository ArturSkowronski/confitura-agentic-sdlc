# Sceny do ilustracji warsztatu

Styl przeniesiony z JVM Weekly (`~/Priv/jvm-weekly/grafiki/prompts.md`), żeby grafiki
warsztatu i newslettera wyglądały jak jedna rodzina. Generuje `tools/illugen.py` w tamtym
repo albo `grafiki/gen.py` tutaj. Referencja postaci: `~/Priv/jvm-weekly/grafiki/ref/avatar.png`
(nie kopiujemy jej do tego repo).

## Style lock

Bold graphic illustration in the manner of a screen print: flat areas of solid
colour, confident ink outlines that vary in weight, and shading done only as
halftone dots or hard-edged shadow shapes. No gradients, no soft glow, no
photographic lighting, no rendered skin. Visible paper grain and a faint
misregistration between the ink layers, the way a risograph misses by half a
millimetre. Each image is printed in four or five inks at most, picked warm and
high-contrast for the scene, plus an off-white paper tone and black for the line.

The character is the attached young developer, redrawn in that flat ink style:
messy light-brown hair, thin red-rimmed glasses, a red polo shirt, black trousers
with a belt. Keep his face shape, hair and red glasses recognisable from the
reference, but simplify him to flat shapes. The red of the polo and the glasses
stays saturated and is always one of the inks. He is the only person in the frame.

Composition is poster-like: one large silhouette carrying the image, strong
diagonals, generous empty paper, every prop reduced to its essential shape and
scale pushed for effect. It has to read at thumbnail size from the silhouette
alone. Warm, confident, dry humour; he is always doing something specific, never
standing and posing. No text unless the scene asks for it.

## Negative

a second person, a crowd, the Java Duke mascot, the Java logo, a green polo shirt,
green glasses frames, any Visdom branding, a wizard staff, photo-realism, 3D render,
smooth gradients, airbrushed shading, lens flare, bokeh, watermarks, blur, extra
limbs, mangled hands, gibberish lettering.

## cover-fabryka

Scene: the floor of a small factory that manufactures software changes. A conveyor
belt runs across the frame carrying identical rectangular cards, each card a tiny
code diff. Above the belt hangs a three-lamp andon tower, only the green lamp lit.
The character stands at a control desk in the foreground, one hand resting on a
large lever, watching the belt rather than the machine. Behind him the hall opens
into darkness with the shapes of more belts. Composition: the belt is a hard
diagonal from lower left to upper right, he is a clean silhouette on the right
third against the lit belt, the andon tower the brightest object in the frame.

## l01-agent

Scene: a yard of stacked shipping containers used as a server room. One container in
the middle is freshly painted and the character is bolting a small enamel nameplate
onto its door, the kind used on an office. A cable runs from the container to a
junction box. The other containers are anonymous and dark. Composition: the lit
container fills the centre, he is small at its door to make it read as a building,
the stack of dark containers rises behind as one mass.

## l02-narzedzia

Scene: a workshop pegboard where exactly five tools hang, each in its painted outline,
nothing else. To the right a heavy steel cabinet with its doors shut and a padlock
through the hasp. The character is turning the key in the padlock with both hands,
leaning his weight into it, while a thin mechanical arm on a rail reaches from the
left towards the cabinet handle and stops short. Composition: pegboard flat and
frontal on the left, cabinet in hard perspective on the right, the reaching arm a
long horizontal that his body interrupts.

## l03-kontekst

Scene: a crossroads with a tall wooden signpost that has two arms pointing down two
different roads. The character stands on a stepladder, both hands on one arm, turning
it from the road that curves away into fog towards the road that runs straight to a
lit building. Around the foot of the signpost stand five open filing drawers of
different heights, like a staircase. Composition: signpost a strong vertical slightly
off centre, the two roads splitting as a wide V behind, drawers a stepped rhythm along
the bottom edge.

## l04-przyjecie

Scene: an intake counter with a metal grille and a small hatch, the kind at a parcel
office. The character stands behind the grille and is pushing a form back out through
the hatch; the form has three large question marks stamped on it in ink. On his side
of the counter a neat stack of accepted forms waits under a paperweight. Composition:
the counter cuts the frame horizontally, he is behind it lit from above, the rejected
form the brightest shape, caught mid-push at the front edge.

## l05-linia

Scene: an assembly line seen from a raised gantry: four stations in a row joined by a
belt, the third station a single robot arm working on a card. The character stands on
the gantry with his forearms on the rail, looking down at the line, a clipboard tucked
under one arm. A cable tray runs above the line. Composition: the line a receding
diagonal into the upper right, he is a foreground silhouette on the left with the rail
cutting across his body, the robot arm the one bright accent along the belt.

## l06-bramki

Scene: a sealed glass inspection booth on the factory floor. Inside the booth a heavy
press stamps a card and the stamp comes down red. The character stands outside the
booth with his hands behind his back, watching through the glass. The same thin
mechanical arm from earlier is pressed flat against the outside of the glass, unable
to reach the press. Composition: the booth a bright lit box in the centre, the red
stamp the focal point, he and the arm both dark shapes against the glass on opposite
sides.

## l07-wyrocznia

Scene: a strongroom wall of small numbered brass doors, like safe deposit boxes. The
character is sliding a sealed wax-stamped envelope into one of the boxes and turning
the key. On the floor beside him a heavier strongbox sits closed, untouched, with two
seals. The mechanical arm reaches into frame from the right but its whole end is
wrapped in a cloth, blinded. Composition: the grid of brass doors fills the back
wall flat and frontal, he is a diagonal figure cutting across the grid, the blinded
arm a dead weight at the right edge.

## l08-ryzyko

Scene: a rework loop: a lower return belt carries a card back towards the start while
the upper belt runs forward, the two belts forming a long closed loop. On the wall a
big round dial with a needle swung hard into the red sector. The character stands at
the point where the return belt meets the line, holding the card up to a large
magnifying lens on an arm. Composition: the two belts a flattened oval filling the
frame, the dial a bright circle upper left, he is at the pinch point of the loop with
the lens enlarging his eye.

## l09-swiatlo

Scene: the same line, now stopped. The andon tower overhead burns yellow and the belt
is still, one card waiting at the stop bar. The character stands at a lectern beside
the line signing his name on a card with a thick pen, unhurried. Behind him a rack
with exactly three slots, two filled, one empty. Composition: the yellow lamp is the
brightest thing and throws the only hard shadow, the stopped belt a flat horizontal,
he is lit from above at the lectern, the three-slot rack a small clear rhythm behind.

## l10-ksiega

Scene: a long paper ledger unrolled across a table and off both edges of the frame,
every entry sealed to the next with a small wax seal so the page reads as a chain.
The character holds the roll up to a lamp at one point where a seal is cracked and the
paper does not line up. His other hand rests flat on the table. Composition: the roll
a hard horizontal band across the frame, the lamp a bright point behind the cracked
seal, he leans in from the right, most of the frame given to the paper.

## l11-karta

Scene: a works notice board on a brick wall. Nine rules are steel plates bolted to the
board with visible rivets, machined and permanent. The tenth is an ordinary paper note
held by a single drawing pin, its corner already curling away from the wall in a
draught. The character stands in front of the board with his hands in his pockets,
head tilted, looking at the paper one. Composition: the board fills most of the frame
flat and frontal, the paper note the only curved shape among rectangles and the only
light value, he is a small dark figure at the lower right for scale.
